Announcements & voice

Spoken from the main overlay when a timer wears off or is dismissed. Also used by verbal expiry warnings below.

Expiry warnings

Flash and verbal share the warn lead time (1–30s). Verbal speaks once when a timer crosses the threshold, using the announcement voice and volume above.

Dismiss & wore off

Right-click dismiss needs overlays unlocked (General → Overlay lock). Recently wore off lists renew-relevant self/ally buffs (Clarity, Celerity, SOW, Chloroplast/regen, shields) — not blossom/celestial HoTs, invis, roots, or enemy debuffs.

Filters & windows

Separate enemy overlay shows debuff/DoT timers on a second window. “Only show my buffs” still keeps your pet when My pet name is set. Use the exact log target for the pet (e.g. Gastik or OwnerName pet).

Respawn window

Leave-zone hides timers in the overlay but they keep counting. Rares use per-camp timers when defined.

Zone & rares

Watched spells

Grouped by class, then level. Enable/Disable all on a class header. Multi-class spells share watch state by name. Upgrade tier comes from the cast log (e.g. “Spirit of Wolf V” → tier 5).

Tracking

Drop rates use observation opportunities: your kills (You have slain / pet when Pet name is set) plus corpses you loot that were not already counted (e.g. group kills). Multiple items from the same corpse count as one opportunity. Zone follows Timers → Respawns / log zone-ins. Requires a saved log path and /log on in-game.

Community sync
