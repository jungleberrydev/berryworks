Character

Level is used for scaled buff durations and auto-updates from log level-ups. Spell Casting Reinforcement extends beneficial spells you cast; invulnerability and combat abilities are exempt.

Overlay lock

When locked, clicks pass through to the game and the OS window outline is hidden. Unlock to drag overlays or right-click dismiss timers.

Announcements & voice

Spoken from the main overlay when a timer wears off or is dismissed. Charm-break and invis-drop alerts use the same voice and also appear on the alert overlay. Also used by verbal expiry warnings below.

Alert overlay

A separate always-on-top window for short messages such as Charm break! and Invis wore off! Unlock overlays to drag it where you want; lock to click through. Size, font, and colors below apply only to this window.

Expiry warnings

Flash and verbal share the warn lead time (1–120 seconds). Type or slide to set how many seconds before expiry. Short timers cap at half their duration so they don’t flash the whole time. Verbal speaks once when a timer crosses the threshold, using the announcement voice and volume above.

Dismiss & wore off

Right-click dismiss needs overlays unlocked (General → Overlay lock). Recently wore off lists renew-relevant self/ally buffs (Clarity, Celerity, SOW, Chloroplast/regen, shields) — not blossom/celestial HoTs, invis, roots, or enemy debuffs.

Filters & windows

Separate enemy overlay shows debuff/DoT timers on a second window. “Only show my buffs” still keeps your pet when My pet name is set. Use the exact log target for the pet (e.g. Gastik or OwnerName pet). Unlock overlays (General) to drag windows — each shows a label while unlocked.

Respawn window

Leave-zone hides timers in the overlay but they keep counting. Rares use per-camp timers when defined.

Zone & rares

Pick your camping zone here, or wait for “You have entered …” in the log.

Watched spells

Grouped by class, then level. Enable/Disable all on a class header. Multi-class spells share watch state by name. Upgrade tier comes from the cast log (e.g. “Spirit of Wolf V” → tier 5).

Theme & colors

Themes set colors, chrome, and (for fantasy/terminal) a matching font. You can still tweak after picking one. Changes apply live to the overlay and this window.

Size & type

Density and font affect all timer overlay windows.

Tracking

Drop rates use observation opportunities: your kills (You have slain / pet when Pet name is set) plus corpses you loot that were not already counted (e.g. group kills). Multiple items from the same corpse count as one opportunity. Zone follows Timers → Respawns / log zone-ins. Requires a saved log path and /log on in-game.

Community sync

Optional uploads to Norrath Roster (norrathroster.com/loot after Discord sign-in. Sign-in opens your browser; uploads are tied to your Discord account (idempotent re-uploads). Community rates on the site may still require the community_loot feature flag.
