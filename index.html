EverQuest Legends toolkit

Log File

Enable logging in-game with /log on, then pick a detected log or Browse to eqlog_<Character>_<Server>.txt.

No logs found in common EverQuest folders — use Browse.

Character

When locked, clicks pass through to the game and the OS window outline is hidden. Unlock to drag overlays or right-click dismiss timers.

Look & feel

Themes set colors and panel opacity; you can still tweak after picking one. Changes apply live.

Timer behavior

Dismiss needs overlays unlocked (General). Recently wore off lists renew-relevant self/ally buffs (Clarity, Celerity, SOW, Chloroplast/regen, shields) — not blossom/celestial HoTs, invis, roots, or enemy debuffs. Voice speaks from the main overlay.

Filters & windows

Separate enemy overlay shows debuff/DoT timers on a second window. “Only show my buffs” still keeps your pet when My pet name is set. Use the exact log target for the pet (e.g. Gastik or OwnerName pet).

Respawn tracking

Pick your camping zone here (or wait for “You have entered …” in the log). Leave zone hides timers but they keep counting. Rares use per-camp timers when defined.

Watched Spells

Spells are grouped by class, then level. Use Enable/Disable all on a class header. Multi-class spells share watch state by name. Upgrade tier comes from the cast log (e.g. “Spirit of Wolf V” → tier 5).
